# BBC Book Selection Criteria

## 1. Purpose

Better Book Club (BBC) independently discovers, evaluates, and recommends books worth readers' attention.

Book selection must be based on clear editorial standards, reliable information, and genuine reading appeal — not simply on an author's request or a book's popularity.

## 2. Where We Discover Books

BBC may discover books through:

- Amazon
- Goodreads
- Publisher websites and catalogues
- Author websites and public author profiles
- Reputable literary publications
- Public book communities and reading platforms
- Other credible sources relevant to book discovery

BBC should actively research and discover suitable books. The process must not depend entirely on authors submitting their own work.

## 3. Core Selection Factors

Every candidate should be assessed using the following factors.

### A. Story Quality and Appeal
- Is the premise interesting?
- Does the story offer something engaging or distinctive?
- Is the description clear and compelling?
- Does the book appear to have a coherent direction?

### B. Writing and Presentation
- Does the available writing sample demonstrate competent storytelling?
- Is the book professionally presented?
- Is the description well written and informative?
- Is there enough publicly available information to evaluate it fairly?

### C. Reader Appeal
- Is there a clear audience for the book?
- Does its genre have an identifiable readership?
- Could it appeal to readers beyond the author's existing audience?

### D. Originality
- Does the book offer an interesting premise, perspective, setting, or approach?
- Does it bring something distinctive to its genre?

### E. Credibility and Verification
- Can the book's title, author, publication status, and other relevant details be verified?
- Are the available sources reliable?
- Are claims about ratings, reviews, rankings, or popularity supported by evidence?

## 4. Genre Considerations

BBC may consider books across different genres, including:

- Fantasy
- Science Fiction
- Romance
- Mystery and Thriller
- Contemporary Fiction
- Literary Fiction
- Historical Fiction
- Adventure
- Horror
- Young Adult
- Nonfiction
- Other suitable literary categories

The catalogue should offer variety rather than repeatedly featuring only one genre.

## Founder-Specified Research Filters (2026-11-27)

The founder has provided the following direction for the initial 50-book research pool:

- Prioritise independently published books for the initial catalogue research. Traditionally published books may be considered when a genuine traditional-publishing client approaches BBC; they are not the focus of this initial independent-book shortlist.
- Use the founder's accepted genres as the starting point: Fantasy, Romance, Science Fiction, Mystery/Thriller, Horror, Literary Fiction, Historical Fiction, Young Adult, and Contemporary. Research may identify adjacent genre categories for the founder to consider; do not silently treat unapproved additions as accepted BBC genres.
- Goodreads should be a key discovery and verification source. The founder's minimum target is 500 Goodreads ratings per candidate where that information is available; the preferred range is approximately 3,000–10,000 ratings.
- Avoid books with extremely large rating counts in the initial candidate pool, particularly titles with millions of ratings, unless an exceptional editorial reason is presented separately.
- Assess the average star rating separately from the number of ratings. Avoid weakly rated books where the rating profile could undermine BBC's editorial credibility. No numeric minimum average rating has been approved; do not invent one.
- Featured Books may use editorial discretion beyond the numerical filters when a title is genuinely compelling and appropriate for BBC. Worth Discovering may include interesting books with lower rating counts, provided quality and reader appeal are supported.
- Strong cover presentation matters, especially for Featured Books. A publicly visible cover is not automatically authorised for reuse; record the source and permission status separately.
- Amazon availability is preferred but not mandatory. Goodreads availability is strongly preferred.
- Assess writing quality, story appeal, originality and reader appeal using available evidence. Ratings and marketing descriptions alone do not prove writing quality.

The initial manual website catalogue is expected to contain 10 approved books. Research should prepare a pool of approximately 50 candidates in advance, stored separately from the public catalogue. The founder will approve which candidates enter the public catalogue. Candidate research must not be inserted into `data/books.json` until approved.

## 5. Popularity Is Not the Only Standard

A book does not need to be a bestseller or have thousands of reviews to qualify.

BBC should consider both:

- Established books with demonstrated reader interest.
- Less widely known books with strong potential and compelling storytelling.

Popularity indicators can inform research, but they must not replace editorial judgment.

## 6. Research and Verification Rules

For every shortlisted book, record as much of the following as can be verified:

- Book title
- Author
- Genre
- Publication status and date
- Official book or publisher page
- Amazon listing, where available
- Goodreads listing, where available
- Rating and review count, where available
- Relevant ranking or popularity indicators, where verifiable
- Cover image source
- Date information was checked
- Reasons for recommending the book
- Any uncertainties or missing information

Never invent ratings, review counts, rankings, sales figures, publication details, or reader engagement.

If information cannot be verified, mark it as unavailable rather than guessing.

## 7. Shortlisting and Approval

The selection process follows these stages:

1. Discover potential books through research.
2. Check basic information and eligibility.
3. Evaluate each candidate against BBC's selection factors.
4. Prepare a shortlist with supporting evidence.
5. Have a second AI independently cross-check important details and assessments.
6. Present the findings for the founder's review.
7. Only add a book to the official featured catalogue after approval.

AI recommendations are advisory. Final selection belongs to the BBC founder.

## 8. Reasons to Exclude or Hold a Book

A candidate may be excluded or placed on hold when:

- Essential book information cannot be verified.
- The book's identity or publication status is unclear.
- There is insufficient material to make a fair assessment.
- The available presentation raises significant quality or credibility concerns.
- The book does not fit BBC's editorial standards.
- There are unresolved copyright or permission concerns relating to materials BBC intends to use.

A lack of popularity alone is not a reason for exclusion.

## 9. Fairness and Editorial Independence

- Do not select books solely because an author asks for a feature.
- Do not allow payment to determine editorial recommendations.
- Do not promise authors selection or favourable reviews.
- Apply consistent standards across authors and publishers.
- Be transparent about the basis of claims made about a book.
- Distinguish editorial recommendations from paid services or promotional opportunities if those are introduced later.

## 10. AI Research Responsibilities

**ChatGPT**
- Discover potential books.
- Research candidates using appropriate public sources.
- Evaluate candidates against these criteria.
- Prepare evidence-backed shortlists.
- Identify missing information and potential concerns.

**Claude**
- Independently cross-check shortlisted books.
- Verify important details against available sources.
- Identify inconsistencies or unsupported claims.
- Help organise approved book information.

**Founder**
- Review recommendations.
- Resolve editorial questions.
- Approve or reject candidates.
- Make final decisions about what BBC features.

## 11. Important Principle

BBC's goal is not simply to collect book titles. It is to build a trustworthy literary discovery platform where every featured book has been considered carefully, every factual claim has a reliable basis, and readers can discover books with confidence.
