## Cover quality standard used

For a potential BBC feature, a cover should:
- Read clearly at thumbnail size, including the title and author name.
- Look professionally composed, with legible typography and a clear visual focal point.
- Signal the book's genre and tone without looking generic or visually cluttered.
- Have a consistent finish and suitable resolution for a book listing.
- Be the correct edition and have a recorded source and permission/licence status before BBC republishes it.

A cover can be aesthetically strong but still be unusable on the BBC website until reuse rights are established. Prefer an author/publisher-supplied press kit or explicit written permission. Do not hotlink retailer image assets as a substitute for permission.
