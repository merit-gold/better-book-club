## How to read this file

This is an evidence-led discovery shortlist, not a final catalogue. Goodreads figures below are snapshots surfaced by the linked Goodreads shelf/list pages on the research date. They can change. A Goodreads shelf or Listopia label is a discovery lead, not conclusive proof of independent publication. Publishing route, exact edition, Amazon availability, cover source/quality, sample writing and rights status still need title-by-title confirmation before a candidate can be marked ready.

The founder clarified on 27 November 2026 that average Goodreads stars should **not** be a rigid gate: a genuinely good book may still qualify. Rating volume matters more as a popularity signal: avoid million-rating phenomena and generally avoid very large rating totals. A modest readership is desirable; the earlier 3,000–10,000 band is not a required target. The earlier 500-rating figure is a useful guide, not an absolute veto where an exceptional discovery merits review.

## Genre expansion research

Royal Road's current fiction search taxonomy includes Action, Adventure, Comedy, Contemporary, Drama, Fantasy, Historical, Horror, Mystery, Psychological, Romance, Satire, Sci-fi, Short Story, Thriller and Tragedy. Its older genre/tag explainer also distinguishes reader-facing story types such as Slice of Life, Martial Arts/Wuxia/Xianxia and related tags. These are useful signals for BBC's genre architecture, not an instruction to adopt every category.

Suggested additions for founder consideration: **Action & Adventure**, **Psychological Fiction**, **Drama**, **Comedy**, **Slice of Life**, and **Short Stories**. Treat Martial Arts/Wuxia/Xianxia as a more specific subgenre/tag rather than a top-level category. Royal Road's categories are a platform taxonomy, not a universal publishing standard.

Sources:
- [Royal Road fiction search and genre filters](https://www.royalroad.com/fictions/search)
- [Royal Road: About Genres and Tags](https://www.royalroad.com/forums/thread/29835)

## Next verification pass

1. Deduplicate and complete the pool to 50 distinct titles.
2. For each title, open the exact Goodreads book page and capture a fresh rating average/count and publication date.
3. Verify independent/self-publishing status through author, publisher/imprint or official book sources rather than relying only on Goodreads shelves.
4. Locate the matching Amazon listing where available; record unavailable rather than guessing.
5. Inspect cover presentation and record its source and reuse permission status. Do not copy covers into the public site without permission or a suitable licence.
6. Review a legitimate sample/excerpt and credible editorial/reader commentary. Do not claim the full book has been read.
7. Mark each title as **keep**, **hold**, or **exclude**, with a short reason. Only then prepare the founder's final review list.
