**Prepared:** 28 November 2026

**Status:** Editorial recommendation for founder review — **not approved for the live catalogue**.

## What this shortlist optimises for

The goal is a first screen of books that makes a visitor curious enough to explore: strong cover art, readable titles at card size, distinct colour palettes, clear genre signals, a compelling premise, and a mix of familiar and less-discovered titles. Goodreads counts are used as context, not as the sole quality measure. The founder's preference is to avoid million-rating phenomena and very large rating totals; a lower average star rating alone is not an automatic rejection.

The cover assessment is about visual impact only. BBC must still obtain permission or a suitable licence for every cover image it republishes. A retailer or Goodreads image being accessible online does not grant reuse rights.

## Important editorial changes before the live 10

The above is a **display shortlist**, not yet a clean ten-book publication list. Three of the visually strongest choices have series-entry concerns:
- *The Drowned Tomb* is book 2.
- *Relic of the Gods* is book 3.
- *Tales from the Gas Station: Volume Two* is book 2.

For a first-time visitor, the better choice is usually the first book in a series. I recommend checking *The Changeling* (James Fahy), *The Echoes Saga* Book I, and *Tales from the Gas Station: Volume One* for equivalent or stronger covers before final approval.

The list is also fantasy/horror-heavy. I have not forced a romance title into the ten simply to tick a genre box: the most visually striking romance cover reviewed, *Sweet Little Hearts*, is published by Montlake/Amazon Publishing and is not a clean independent-publishing fit for BBC's initial indie-first pool. If the founder wants one traditional-publishing exception for genre balance, it can be considered separately.

## Cover rights and asset handling

- Use author/publisher press-kit files or obtain written permission for the exact edition cover.
- Do not download and republish retailer/Goodreads thumbnails just because they are publicly accessible.
- Record edition ISBN, cover source, rights holder, permission/licence and permission date.
- Use a high-resolution portrait image with enough resolution for responsive cards.
- Check the cover at small mobile-card size; if title/author cannot be read, do not use it as a hero feature.
- No cover images have been added to the public website.
