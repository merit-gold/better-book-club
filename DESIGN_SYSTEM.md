# Better Book Club — Design System

## 1. Design Vision

Better Book Club should feel like a premium literary destination: elegant, welcoming, modern, distinctive, and trustworthy.

The website must not look like a generic blog, an ordinary book listing template, or an automatically generated website.

The design should communicate that BBC is a carefully curated literary community.

## 2. Brand Identity

- Brand name: Better Book Club
- Short name: BBC
- Existing BBC logo: Must be retained exactly as provided.
- Do not redesign, distort, recolour, or replace the existing logo without the founder's approval.
- The logo should remain recognisable and consistent across the website.

## 3. Colour Palette

These are the working brand colours:

| Colour | Hex | Intended Use |
|---|---|---|
| Burgundy | #8B1E3F | Primary brand colour, important buttons and accents |
| Forest Green | #174B40 | Secondary brand colour, selected accents |
| Warm Ivory | #FFF8EF | Main background and light sections |
| Soft Rose | #F1DCE2 | Subtle highlights and supporting sections |
| Copper | #C58A52 | Small decorative accents and details |
| Charcoal | #292326 | Main text and dark sections |

Use the colours with restraint. The website should feel balanced and refined, not overly colourful.

The palette may be adjusted during visual testing, but significant changes require founder approval.

## 4. Typography

Use an elegant serif typeface for major headings and a clean, readable sans-serif typeface for body text.

Suggested direction:

- Headings: Playfair Display or a comparable literary serif.
- Body text: Inter, Lato, or a comparable sans-serif.
- Buttons and navigation: Clean sans-serif typography.

The Foundation Skeleton uses system serif/sans-serif font stacks with no third-party font requests. Self-hosted fonts may be added later.

Typography must remain readable on desktop and mobile devices.

Avoid excessive font sizes, overly decorative text, and inconsistent heading styles.

## 5. Layout and Spacing

- Use generous spacing to create a calm, premium appearance.
- Maintain consistent content widths and alignment.
- Use clear visual hierarchy.
- Avoid overcrowded sections.
- Keep navigation simple and easy to understand.
- Ensure sections transition naturally.
- Use responsive layouts across screen sizes.

## 6. Homepage Design

The homepage should introduce BBC immediately and make book discovery the main attraction.

Expected sections include:

1. Header and navigation.
2. Hero section with a clear BBC introduction and strong visual identity.
3. Featured Books.
4. Explore Books or Discover Your Next Read.
5. Popular or Noteworthy Books, with properly sourced claims.
6. Coming Soon.
7. Reader and author opportunities.
8. About BBC.
9. Newsletter subscription.
10. Footer with useful navigation and contact information.

The exact layout may evolve during implementation.

## 7. Book Cards

Book cards should make covers visually prominent.

Each card may include:

- Book cover.
- Title.
- Author.
- Genre.
- Short editorial introduction or description.
- Feature label where appropriate.
- Link to the individual book page.

Cards should have consistent proportions and spacing.

Avoid excessive text that makes the catalogue difficult to scan.

## 8. Individual Book Pages

Each featured book should have its own page.

The page should provide:

- Prominent book cover.
- Title and author.
- Genre and relevant metadata.
- Original BBC editorial introduction.
- Book description, with appropriate sourcing.
- Feature date where relevant.
- External links to legitimate book pages.
- Relevant author information where available.

Individual pages should feel like editorial book profiles, not simple product listings.

## 9. Navigation

The main navigation should be clear and consistent.

Suggested structure:

- Home
- Explore Books
- For Authors
- For Readers
- About
- Contact

Newsletter signup is placed on the homepage and in the footer, not in the main navigation.

The Explore Books area may contain categories such as Featured Books, Recommendations, Coming Soon, and Past Features.

The private administration dashboard must not appear as a public navigation item.

## 10. Buttons and Interactive Elements

Buttons should have consistent styling and clear labels.

Examples:

- Explore Books
- Discover More
- Submit a Book
- Join the Community
- Subscribe

Use subtle hover and focus states.

All interactive elements must work. Do not create decorative buttons that have no actual function.

## 11. Images and Book Covers

- Use high-quality, authorised or appropriately licensed images.
- Preserve original book-cover proportions.
- Do not stretch or distort covers.
- Avoid low-resolution or visibly pixelated assets.
- Use consistent image treatment throughout the catalogue.
- Provide meaningful alternative text for important images.

Public availability of a cover image does not automatically grant permission to reuse it.

## 12. Responsive Design

The website must work well on:

- Desktop computers.
- Laptops.
- Tablets.
- Mobile phones.

Mobile design must not simply shrink the desktop layout.

Navigation, book cards, typography, forms, and spacing must adapt to smaller screens.

## 13. Accessibility

The design should follow good accessibility practices:

- Readable text sizes.
- Sufficient colour contrast.
- Keyboard-accessible navigation.
- Visible focus states.
- Descriptive labels for form fields.
- Alternative text for meaningful images.
- Clear error and success messages.
- Semantic page structure.

## 14. Motion and Effects

Use subtle transitions and animations where they improve the experience.

Avoid excessive motion, distracting effects, and unnecessary animation.

Respect reduced-motion preferences where practical.

## 15. Visual Quality Standards

Before accepting a design or page, check:

- Does it look polished and professionally designed?
- Does it feel like a literary brand?
- Is the existing BBC logo preserved?
- Are the book covers prominent?
- Is the typography consistent?
- Are colours balanced?
- Is the layout responsive?
- Are there any awkward gaps, overlaps, or alignment problems?
- Do buttons and interactive elements work?
- Does the design feel cohesive across the website?
