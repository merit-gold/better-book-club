BETTER BOOK CLUB / FOR READERS

A note to readers

✳

There is always another story to discover.

Better Book Club is an independent literary discovery and reader community. We want to help readers come across books they might not otherwise find—and make space for genuine reader perspectives.

You do not need to be a professional critic to have a worthwhile point of view. A thoughtful response begins with your honest experience of a book.

What matters here

Read with curiosity.
Respond with honesty.

Reader participation should be welcoming, voluntary, and respectful of every person's genuine opinion.

01

Your honest voice

We value genuine reactions. You are never expected to praise a book or change your opinion to please an author or BBC.

02

Participation by choice

Reading opportunities are invitations, not obligations. You should be able to decide whether a particular book and activity suit you.

03

Respect for the work

Respect authors' rights, protect private or unpublished material, and treat fellow readers and contributors with care.

01 / Ways to take part

Different ways to be
part of the story.

01Planned opportunity

Founding Readers

A starting circle of readers who can help shape the spirit of the community as Better Book Club grows. The practical details and participation expectations will be shared before this opportunity opens.

02Planned opportunity

Beta Reading

Opportunities to read eligible work and share considered feedback with an author. Any future arrangement will explain the reading brief, timing, confidentiality expectations, and how feedback is to be provided.

02 / The reader experience

Room for your
own perspective.

01

Find a book that interests you

Explore the BBC catalogue and keep an eye out for future reader opportunities.

02

If an opportunity is available, review its details and decide freely whether it works for you.

03

Where feedback is part of an activity, it should reflect your real reading experience—not a requested verdict.

A few useful answers

Before you
join the conversation.

We want expectations to be clear before any reader opportunity begins.

Can I join a reader programme now?

Not yet. Founding Readers, Beta Reading, and ARC opportunities are planned, but their operating details are still being prepared. There is no reader application form open at present.

Does it cost anything to be a reader?

Reader participation is free. Any specific opportunity will explain what is involved before you decide whether to take part.

Do I have to leave a positive review?

No. BBC values honest opinions and does not require positive reviews, favourable ratings, or a particular response.

Will I receive a book for every opportunity?

We cannot promise a book or a match for every reader. Availability will depend on the details of each future opportunity.

What if a book or manuscript is confidential?

Any opportunity involving unpublished or private material will need clear confidentiality expectations. Readers should not share or distribute material without permission.

Do I need to be a professional reviewer?

No professional-reviewer status is promised or required by the current reader programme outline. What matters is respectful, genuine participation and following the rules of the specific opportunity.
