BETTER BOOK CLUB / FOR AUTHORS

A note to authors

✳

We’re interested in the stories that stay with people.

BBC is an independent literary discovery and reader community. We look beyond what is already everywhere, seeking books that offer readers something worth their time.

Authors and publishers are welcome to introduce us to their work. A submission gives us a chance to consider a book; it does not buy a place in the collection or replace our independent editorial judgement.

What guides us

Good relationships
begin with honesty.

We want authors to understand what BBC does, what it does not promise, and how decisions are made.

01

Independent discovery

We research books independently and welcome suitable submissions. The work should speak for itself, not arrive with a promise of attention.

02

Thoughtful consideration

We consider a book against our editorial approach and the interests of the readers we are building connections with.

03

Honest outcomes

No guaranteed features, reader matches, positive reviews, ratings, sales or exposure. Reader opinions must remain their own.

OUR COMMITMENT TO TRANSPARENCY

03 / Trust and ownership

Your work
remains yours.

Authors and publishers retain ownership of their original work. Sending a book for consideration does not transfer copyright to Better Book Club.

We use book covers, descriptions and excerpts responsibly and seek appropriate permission where it is required. Authors should only share materials they own or are authorised to provide.

If a reader opportunity is ever arranged, participation and feedback must remain genuine. No author can purchase a positive opinion, rating or review.

A few useful answers

Before you
get in touch.

Clear expectations matter to us. Here are some of the questions authors may have.

Does submitting a book guarantee a feature?

No. Every book is considered independently, and the founder makes the final selection decision. A submission does not guarantee a feature or publication.

What are the costs of working with BBC?

The website submission route and author service arrangements are not finalised yet. Any future service would have a clearly explained scope and price before agreement. Payment would never guarantee editorial selection.

Can I pay for a positive review or rating?

No. BBC does not sell positive reviews or ratings, and readers are never required to give favourable feedback.

Will BBC find a reader for my book?

We cannot guarantee a reader match. If a suitable opportunity exists, the reader is asked whether they want to participate and may accept or decline.

Can I send an unpublished manuscript now?

Please do not send unpublished manuscripts through this website. The website submission route is not available yet, and any future manuscript-sharing process will need clear confidentiality arrangements.

Who makes the final decision?

The founder retains final authority over official BBC book selections and features.
