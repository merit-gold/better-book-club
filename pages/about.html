BETTER BOOK CLUB / OUR STORY
01 / Our purpose ✳

We believe finding a book should feel like finding something.

Better Book Club is an independent literary discovery and reader community. We make room for considered recommendations, new voices and the joy of finding a story that feels like it was meant for you.

There are more books available than any of us could ever read. BBC exists to make that abundance feel less overwhelming and more inviting — with thoughtful curation and editorial presentation that help readers decide what to pick up next.

02 / Our founder & why we exist

Built from a belief in the connection between books and readers.

I'm Aliyah Merit-gold, the founder of Better Book Club and a Literary Growth Partner. I've spent more than nine years in the book space, connecting authors with readers and helping authors think about how their work can be discovered by the people most likely to enjoy it.

I created BBC after seeing how often authors struggle to find visibility, genuine reader engagement and honest feedback on their work. At the same time, readers are surrounded by more books than they can reasonably explore. I wanted to build a thoughtful bridge between those needs: a place where books can be discovered through considered recommendations, features and book updates, and where readers can find stories that genuinely interest them.

Our approach is built around discovery and meaningful connection, not promises of sales, guaranteed attention or positive reviews. Newsletters are part of the way we intend to keep readers connected as that channel becomes available.

03 / How we discover

Curiosity first.
Care at every step.

Our approach is independent, considered and guided by a simple principle: the details matter.

01

We look beyond the obvious.

We independently research books through suitable public sources and remain open to discovering stories from different authors, genres and perspectives.

↗
02

We consider each book on its merits.

Books are evaluated against BBC's selection criteria. A submission can introduce us to a book, but it does not guarantee a feature.

✳
03

We value accuracy and fairness.

We aim to present book and author information accurately, respect copyright, and distinguish our own editorial perspective from sourced information.

◎
04

Editorial decisions stay independent.

Research can inform a shortlist, but the founder retains final authority over which books are selected and featured.

✦
