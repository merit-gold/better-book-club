# AUTHOR AND READER POLICIES

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the principles and policies governing how Better Book Club (BBC) interacts with authors, publishers, readers, and other members of the literary community.

BBC aims to create a trustworthy, respectful, and welcoming environment for discovering books and connecting readers with stories.

These policies will be reviewed and expanded as the platform develops.

## 2. Author Participation

Authors and publishers may interact with BBC through opportunities such as:

- Book submissions.
- Book feature consideration.
- Author interviews or conversations.
- Promotional collaborations.
- Other literary activities introduced by BBC.

Participation requirements may vary depending on the activity.

Submitting a book does not automatically guarantee selection, publication, promotion, or a feature.

## 3. Book Selection and Editorial Independence

BBC maintains editorial independence over its book selections.

- Books may be discovered independently by BBC or submitted by authors and publishers.
- Every book must meet the applicable selection criteria.
- Authors cannot demand selection or influence editorial decisions through pressure.
- BBC may decline a submission without publishing a detailed explanation.
- Final selection and publication decisions remain with BBC.

Any paid promotional arrangement introduced in the future must be clearly distinguished from independent editorial selection.

## 4. Author Responsibilities

Authors participating in BBC activities are expected to:

- Provide accurate information about their books and themselves.
- Ensure they have the necessary rights to submitted materials.
- Respect readers, other authors, and BBC representatives.
- Avoid submitting misleading ratings, reviews, rankings, or achievements.
- Respect agreed deadlines and participation requirements.
- Notify BBC when submitted information needs correction.

## 5. Reader Responsibilities

Readers participating in BBC activities are expected to:

- Treat authors and fellow readers respectfully.
- Provide honest opinions and feedback.
- Avoid harassment, threats, or abusive communication.
- Respect copyright and avoid unauthorized distribution of books.
- Follow the rules of any reader programme they voluntarily join.

Readers are not required to provide positive reviews or favourable opinions.

## 6. Reviews, Ratings, and Feedback

BBC supports honest reader opinions.

- Reviews and feedback must reflect genuine reading experiences.
- BBC does not require positive reviews.
- No participant should be pressured to change an honest opinion.
- BBC does not guarantee reviews, ratings, or favourable reader responses.
- Any future review programme must comply with applicable platform rules and laws.

## 7. Copyright and Intellectual Property

Authors and publishers retain ownership of their original work unless a separate written agreement states otherwise.

Submitting a book does not transfer copyright to BBC.

BBC must obtain appropriate permission before using copyrighted materials where permission is required.

Authors should not submit materials they do not own or have authorization to share.

BBC's original editorial content, branding, website design, and other original materials are protected by applicable intellectual property laws.

## 8. Privacy and Confidentiality

BBC will handle personal information responsibly.

- Personal information should only be collected for legitimate platform purposes.
- Private author communications and unpublished materials must not be shared publicly without permission, except where disclosure is legally required.
- Access to personal information should be limited to people who need it for their responsibilities.
- Any reader or author programme involving personal information must have clear privacy rules.

A separate privacy policy should be published before the website begins collecting personal information from the public.

## 9. Promotional Opportunities and Fees

BBC may introduce promotional services, paid features, partnerships, or other opportunities in the future.

Until those arrangements are formally approved:

- No specific price or fee should be promised.
- No feature should be described as guaranteed.
- No one should be told that payment guarantees editorial selection.
- Any approved paid service must clearly explain its price, scope, requirements, and limitations.

All promotional claims must be truthful and must not promise guaranteed sales, readers, reviews, ratings, rankings, or publishing success.

## 10. Reader Programmes

BBC may introduce structured reader programmes in the future.

Before launching any programme, BBC must establish:

- Eligibility requirements.
- Participation expectations.
- Confidentiality requirements where applicable.
- Feedback and review guidelines.
- Communication procedures.
- Any rewards, benefits, or limitations.

No reader programme should be advertised as active until its operating rules and arrangements are ready.

## 11. Communication and Conduct

BBC aims to maintain professional, friendly, and respectful communication.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation, deliberate misinformation, spam, and abusive behaviour may result in restricted participation or removal from BBC activities.

BBC may take reasonable action to protect its community and reputation.

## 12. Corrections and Complaints

Authors and readers may contact BBC to report:

- Incorrect book information.
- Copyright or ownership concerns.
- Privacy concerns.
- Inappropriate conduct.
- Other issues relating to BBC activities.

BBC will review reports and make reasonable efforts to correct verified errors.

## 13. Policy Changes

BBC may update these policies as its services and community develop.

Material changes should be communicated through appropriate website notices or other relevant channels.

## 14. Final Authority

The founder and authorized BBC administrators retain responsibility for final operational, editorial, and policy decisions.

No AI assistant, contributor, or external collaborator may independently establish or change official BBC policies without approval.
